Why Does Tuborg Beer Price Differ by State?

Alcohol pricing in India is influenced by state excise policies, taxes, distribution costs and local retail rules. As a result, the same product can have different prices in different states.

Bottle size and variant also affect the final price. For state information, visit the Delhi Excise Department or the relevant official excise department for your state. Use the MRP printed on the actual package as your primary reference.
